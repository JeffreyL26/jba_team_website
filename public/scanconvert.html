About

August 30, 2026

ScanConvert is finally here to make your travels a whole lot easier! After closed testing, we have hit the Play Store with our first app. Check it out and give us feedback!

Our mission is to make going abroad even better. ScanConvert is something we have always wished for when having to search for currency conversions in other countries online. No internet connection? Never a problem again. And don't worry, iOS users - help is near!

Features

  • Scan prices and get the conversion in the currency of your choice!
  • Currency of choice? We have virtually any currency!
  • Internet connection lost? No problem - the last rate we fetched is saved!
  • Scanner fails (rare occasion)? First, take a screenshot and send it to us. Second, just enter it manually using the keyboard icon at the bottom right!
  • Planned your whole trip in your home currency? You might lose track of your spending in a foreign currency. ScanConvert has lists where you can set a budget in your home currency and add your scans on your trip - showing you how much you have spent in YOUR money!
