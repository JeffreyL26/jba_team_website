ScanConvert

Travel Tool · Android

Point your camera at any price and instantly see what it costs in your money.

No typing, no mental math at the market stall. ScanConvert reads prices live through the camera, converts them on the spot across 166 currencies, and lets you collect what you spend in travel budget lists. Recognition runs fully on-device and the last rates are cached, so it keeps working with zero signal, exactly where you need it: abroad.
